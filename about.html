About

The heart behind D'Marie Vivre

Founder and lead facilitator

Mary Okunloye (Ms. Mary)

Educational Psychologist and Consultant, educator, curriculum developer and parent coach.

Ms. Mary founded D'Marie Vivre on one conviction: that every child carries a unique light, and the right environment, approach and caring educator can help it shine. Today she leads the Hub's programmes, workshops and celebrations.
