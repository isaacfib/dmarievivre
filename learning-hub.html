Good to know

Questions parents ask

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm and Saturday 9am to 4pm.

How much is KidzCrib Regular?

KidzCrib Regular is for ages 1 to 5, Monday to Friday, 9am to 2pm, at ₦190,000 per term.

What does Afterschool cost?

Afterschool runs 3pm to 5pm, Monday to Friday, for ages 3 to 12. It is ₦5,000 daily, ₦30,000 monthly or ₦85,000 per term. Logistics such as school pickup are priced by location, and parents collect from 5pm.

Is there a sibling discount?

Yes. Families with 3 or more children enrolled get 10% off on any programme.

How does KidzCrib Premium work?

A facilitator teaches your child at your home from 9am to 12noon on days that suit your family. Fees are quoted per family, monthly, 6-monthly or yearly.

Can adults learn at the Hub?

Yes. Adult Learning offers Literacy, Numeracy and General Study, Monday to Friday 10am to 1pm, for 3 months. A certificate is awarded and a payment plan is available. The next course starts in January 2027.

Are the celebrations free?

Yes. The Hub's celebrations, such as Women's Day, Book Day, Children's Day and the Christmas Party, are free. See the 2027 Calendar for dates.

How do I enrol?

Send an enquiry on the website, call or WhatsApp, or visit the Hub. We will confirm availability, fees and the start date with you.

Also available at the Hub

Free celebrations through the year, hire of the Hub space on Saturdays, training for school staff and the Hub Bookshop.
