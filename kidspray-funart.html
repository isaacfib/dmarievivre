About the Event

Why Fun Art
matters

KidsPray Fun Art is D'Marie Vivre's biggest gathering of the year — a day where creativity and faith meet. Children paint alongside new friends, sing, dance, and take part in a prayer experience built just for them.

It's part celebration, part classroom, and part church picnic — and every year it grows. Follow along on Instagram and WhatsApp so you never miss registration when it opens.

Made for Children

Every activity — from painting to dance — is designed around how children actually learn and play, led by patient, experienced facilitators.

Rooted in Faith

Bible songs and a group prayer moment sit right alongside the art and play — faith isn't an add-on, it's woven through the whole day.

A Growing Tradition

What started as a single gathering has become an annual highlight families look forward to. This page will grow with it, year after year.
